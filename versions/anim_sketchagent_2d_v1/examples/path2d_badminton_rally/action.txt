Side view of a full-width badminton court. Left player stands at the far left end, facing +x; right player at the far right end, facing −x; a tall net at x=0 divides the court. Beat 1: left player strikes the shuttle at the left end. Beat 2: shuttle arcs high over the net, traveling +x across the long empty middle while both players recover at their ends. Beat 3: right player strikes the shuttle at the right end. Beat 4: shuttle arcs high back over the net traveling −x while both recover. Beat 5: left player meets the returning shuttle with the racket again at the left end — a completed two-way rally. Only the shuttle and the players' limbs move; feet stay planted at their court ends, net and ground stay put.
