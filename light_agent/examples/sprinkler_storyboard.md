## Action

A person notices a fire on the right, runs to the alarm on the left, and pulls its handle. Only after the pull finishes does the overhead sprinkler start spraying. The water visibly reaches the flames before they shrink and disappear. The person turns toward the wet, extinguished spot and relaxes.

## Notes

Fixed wide camera; stable character and scenery proportions; large, recognizable alarm handle and sprinkler; visible water path from sprinkler to flames. Show the fire as flames, not an unidentified small prop.
